# Giselle Ulloa bilingual technology brand

## Goal
Build a Spanish-first, fully bilingual digital identity that immediately positions Giselle across BUILD, CONNECT, CREATE, and SHARE—without reading like an agency, résumé, or invented portfolio.

## Experience
- Create `/es` and `/en` home experiences with equivalent localized navigation, copy, calls to action, forms, validation, image descriptions, and metadata.
- Add localized pages for Build, Connect, Create, Speak, Projects, About, Contact, and the four commercial solution areas.
- Keep `ES | EN` visible, switch to the equivalent page, remember the selection, and send `/` to Spanish by default.
- Use a mobile-first editorial layout with a typographic GISSELLE ULLOA wordmark, GU monogram, concise navigation, and restrained motion.

## Content and visuals
- Build the requested home narrative: identity, four pillars, commercial solutions, problem paths, editable work placeholders, credentials, speaking, community, consulting, contact, and final CTA.
- Use authentic editable placeholders where projects, photos, event details, affiliations, or links are unverified; hide empty proof sections.
- Treat ANDICOM 2026 only as stated panel participation, never as an award or EU representation.
- Create a sophisticated abstract editorial technology composition rather than fake client screenshots or generic AI imagery.

## Technical approach
- Use one central typed localization/data system and reusable components rather than duplicated language pages.
- Implement locale-aware route helpers, equivalent-page language switching, semantic landmarks, keyboard support, accessible labels, reduced motion, and client-side contact form validation.
- Define the supplied palette and Space Grotesk / Inter / JetBrains Mono typography as semantic design tokens.
- Give every localized route unique translated SEO metadata, canonical and hreflang links, Open Graph text, and sitemap-ready URLs.

## Validation
- Verify build diagnostics and inspect the finished experience in a real browser at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Check language switching, route coverage, translated states, form behavior, contrast, overflow, and navigation continuity.
